# IC Copilot v0.2.0

The second public edition separates useful completed research from research
that has passed current publication-quality gates.

## Highlights

- Deterministic validation now precedes the independent frozen-artifact audit.
- Typed publication failures compile into bounded, idempotent repair actions.
- Every material audit finding requires an accepted revision, an evidenced
  rejection, or an explicit unresolved response.
- New evidence invalidates affected conclusions, semantic scores, and closure.
- Quantified analogs count independent source origins rather than URLs.
- Coverage-profile gates support operating companies, banks/insurers, REITs,
  commodity producers, ADR/FPIs, pre-revenue companies, and event research.
- Audit includes a readable Publication readiness view and Repair and recheck
  workflow; routine UI contains no raw hashes or internal IDs.
- The AAPL demo freezer evaluates candidates newest first and preserves the
  existing public artifact when no candidate passes unchanged quality gates.

## Compatibility

Historical manifests and demos remain readable. Older completed research may
be currently ineligible under a newer policy without rewriting its original
audit or evidence graph.

## Integrity promise

Models can request or discover candidates but cannot admit evidence. Only
registered retrieval followed by the common admission gateway can create an
evidence ID. A failed repair remains an inspectable research draft and never
inherits a QA-passed or publication-quality label.
